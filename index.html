Chocolate Cake

A soft, rich chocolate cake layered with silky frosting and built for beginners who want a reliable, crowd-friendly dessert.

Prep Time 45 mins
Bake Time 35 mins
Serves 8 slices

What You Need

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 cups caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup hot coffee

How To Make It

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 180 C and grease two 8-inch cake tins.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, and baking powder in a large mixing bowl.
  3. Add eggs, milk, and oil, then mix until the batter becomes smooth.
  4. Pour in the hot coffee and stir gently to loosen the batter.
  5. Divide between tins and bake for 30 to 35 minutes until springy.
  6. Cool completely, frost generously, and slice for serving.
